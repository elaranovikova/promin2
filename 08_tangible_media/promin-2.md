## Description text

> An aluminum card used for the Promin and Promin-2, the first Soviet transistor
> computer. The Promin computer was intended for engineers and was conceived in
> 1958 by Viktor Mikhailovich Glushkov and designed by S.B. Pogrebinsky in the
> early 1960s. It was the first Soviet computer to use microcode.
